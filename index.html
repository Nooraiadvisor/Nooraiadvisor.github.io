The Problem

Subject choice is broken — and schools know it.

Students make life-defining decisions with one meeting, a PDF booklet, and a guess. The current system is failing them — and counsellors.

😕

Students guess, not choose

Most teens pick subjects based on friends or "what seems easier" — not a clear link to their strengths or future careers.

⏳

Counsellors are stretched

One counsellor supports hundreds of students — repeating the same basic conversations instead of doing meaningful guidance.

📄

Static tools for dynamic choices

Subject options live in long PDFs. There's no interactive way to test the fit between a student's profile and their future options.

📊

Leadership has no visibility

School leaders can't easily see which cohorts are confident, which domains are in demand, or who still needs support.

Our Solution

Meet Noor — your school's AI guidance companion.

Noor means "light" in Arabic. Our mission is to illuminate every student's path — using AI, data, and personalized learning.

  • 1

    AI-guided conversation

    Students chat with Noor in a friendly, structured way and receive a personalised Study Pathway Card — subjects, career themes, and key skills.

  • 2

    Career domains that feel real

    Noor groups futures into teen-friendly domains (Tech Wizards, Creators & Storytellers, Mind & Health Heroes) — not a long job list.

  • 3

    Gamified skills journeys

    Short 10-minute missions, badges, and progress bars — each tied to the 11 employability skills students will actually need.

  • 4

    Counsellor dashboard

    Counsellors see who's ready, who needs help, and which career domains are trending — without extra admin work.
